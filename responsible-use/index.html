Bring your own content

Nyanime is a general-purpose player and reader. It does not host or distribute unauthorized anime, manga, television programmes or other copyrighted works. You bring your own content, local files and compatible extensions.

Use only material you are legally entitled to access, such as your own original work, public-domain material, or content made available with the necessary permission or licence. Extensions and developer examples

The application supplies generic APIs. An independently installed extension supplies its own catalogue, parsing rules and content references. Compatibility or installation does not certify the lawfulness of a source or grant rights to its content. Extensions must not be used to infringe copyright.

The separate developer starter uses original synthetic video, geometric manga pages and sample articles. Its demo media are dedicated under CC0-1.0, with branding and font licences identified separately. No third-party scans, episodes or streams are needed to learn the APIs.

The application's software licence governs its code. It does not grant rights to independently supplied media, cover artwork, metadata or trademarks.
