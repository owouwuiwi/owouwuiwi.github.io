Good to know

A few questions.

Does Nyanime include content?

No. Nyanime is a player and reader, rather than a content hosting service. You choose what to watch or read; access and availability depend on the content you use.

Do I need an account?

You do not need a Nyanime account. Optional tracking services and some sources may require their own sign-in. For rooms, share an invite and connect to the relays.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. A .nyabk backup includes your library, progress, history, and settings, excluding downloaded media. A complete backup may contain credentials, so keep it somewhere safe.

Where does Nyanime come from?

Nyanime is a fork of AniYomi and builds on the work of Mihon, Tachiyomi, and their contributors. Find project information, component licences and credits in the documentation.
