Good to know

A few questions.

Does Nyanime encourage piracy?+

Nyanime does not promote piracy in any way or form. It does not host or distribute unauthorized works. Respect creators, copyright, licences and access conditions. Read our bring-your-own-content policy.

Does Nyanime include content?+

Nyanime follows a bring-your-own-content model. It provides a player and reader; you bring local files and compatible extensions. Use only content you are legally entitled to access.

Do I need an account?+

You do not need a Nyanime account. Optional tracking services and some sources may require their own sign-in. For rooms, share an invite and connect to the relays.

Can I back up my data?+

Yes. A .nyabk backup includes your library, progress, history and settings, excluding downloaded media. A complete backup may contain credentials, so keep it somewhere safe. Backups can also bring your data to another device.

Where does Nyanime come from?+

Nyanime is a fork of AniYomi and builds on the work of Mihon, Tachiyomi and their contributors. Read the licensing information for the application source and its independently licensed components.
